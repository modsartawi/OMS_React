The anatomy of a result line

Every line, number and promotion below is read from 098's captured payloads — nothing is invented. The device is 101's: no status-dot column, no count banner, the promotions rail beside the results. What is being ruled here is the line — which columns earn width, how money reads, how a W line behaves, what marks the selected line, and whether the table foots itself.

Ruled by the owner, 2026-07-25 — the defaults below are the approved line: column set B, the verdict (was / saved, blank on an undiscounted line); a W line suppresses its money and says not priced; no totals row (the strip already carries it, and the captures prove they agree); a hand-entered condition is marked with a neutral MANUAL chip. The other switch positions are kept as the rejected candidates, drawn on the same captures.

What the captures proved before a pixel was drawn

ClaimEvidence
Today's money column order does not follow the arithmetic The chain on every line is gross + promo = net, then net + tax = netTotal. Today's grid prints subtotal(netValue) · promo · gross · tax · net(netTotal) — the post-discount figure sits before the discount and the pre-discount figure after it. Verified on all 9 priced captures.
Tax carries no per-line information 15.0% of net on every line of every capture (27.38/182.52, 17.80/118.64, 11.50/76.70 …). A column that is the same multiple everywhere is a column the eye can reconstruct.
On an undiscounted line, gross ≡ net Every line of 01, 03 #20, 04b #20. So a plain line spends 5 money columns on 2 independent numbers.
The lines do foot to the header — exactly Σ netTotal, Σ promotionDiscount, Σ grossValue and Σ taxValue equal the header's to the penny on all 9 captures. 098's "different pennies" is a cross-run artefact, not an intra-run one.
A discount is always negative, never positive −63.88, −41.30, −41.48, −43.30, −105.18. The sign is real, so it is printed; but it is not an error, so it is not red.
A W line is all zeros, not a small number 04b #10 COUP01: netPrice, gross, net, tax, netTotal all 0, one message, one condition. Printing five 0.00s says "priced at zero"; it did not price at all.
One promotion spans lines 05: BBY 000100000131 against items 10 and 20 (materials 200706, 200710), one summed discount. The cross-highlight is the only thing on screen that says so.
A manual condition is invisible on the line today 06 #10 carries ZB01 −5.00 with conditionOrigin: 'M' folded into the same promotionDiscount as the bonus buy. 098 finding 6 makes a silent manual condition the difference between an explicable run and a 400.
