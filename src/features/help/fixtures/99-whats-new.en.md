Every change to this help, newest first. Each article also says which release of the screens it was checked
against.

## 22 November 2026: the whole of Analytics is covered {#2026-11-22-full}

The help now has an article for every part of Analytics:

- [Schedules](help:schedules): sending a report by email, what arrives (and the email that comes ["without
  charts"](help:schedules#no-charts)), the kept runs, and **My schedules**.
- [Sharing and certified items](help:sharing-certified): the three tiers, sharing, narrowing, handing over, and what
  certification means.
- [The data catalog](help:catalog): areas, measures, dimensions, the tables behind them, previews and printing.
- [Copilot rules and what it knows about you](help:copilot-admin): what the house rules can and cannot change, the
  starter questions, and the readings the Copilot keeps for you.
- [SQL](help:sql): asking in SQL mode, the SQL editor, and how SQL items are marked.
- [Item lists](help:item-lists): uploading a list, its versions, and asking the Copilot about it.
- [Price command center](help:price-command-center): the index, price alerts, the grid, matching and feed health.
- [Scheduling by asking](help:copilot#schedule) now says that a schedule you ask for needs only viewer access.

## 22 November 2026: the help is published {#2026-11-22}

- The Analytics help is published, in English and Arabic, marked **Draft** until it is approved.
- First articles: [Start here](help:start), with the menu, the [four access levels](help:start#access), how to get
  access, and the status card.
- The reading articles: [Home and the library](help:home-library), [Views](help:views), [Boards](help:boards),
  [Reports](help:reports) and [Ask the OMS](help:copilot).
- [Start here](help:start) now also says [what the data covers](help:start#coverage) and how to learn the first
  tasks while the [guided tours](help:start#tours) are not available.
- The four [guided tours](help:start#tours) are written: **First look around**, **Build your first board**, **Your
  first report** and **Ask the Copilot**. They start from the app once that part of it is released.
