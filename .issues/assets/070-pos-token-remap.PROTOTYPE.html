070 — The POS token remap (light)

Prototype for ticket 070 of map 068. Proposes the exact light-mode value for every semantic token once PosTheme.xaml is the standard, and shows the swap against two existing screens. Nothing is applied — src/app/global.css is untouched; /to-spec carries the approved table into the spec.

The four decisions this ticket owes

DecisionProposalWhy
--primary
the genuine choice		 #2F63A6 — POS --accent, not --key. POS --key #586674 is the same value as --ink-2, which becomes our 358-use --muted-foreground. Mapping --primary there would paint every default button the exact colour of dim body text. All 126 --primary call sites are actions or accents — filled buttons (17), text-primary on active tabs / badges / bullets (33), border-primary on selected rows (26), bg-primary/10 tints (15). Not one is body ink, so the steel blue lands as an improvement on every one of them. POS --key still gets a home — see --secondary below.
Colour space Hex, replacing the current oklch(). Three reasons, all practical: (1) the values stay byte-verifiable against PosTheme.xaml — a reviewer can diff them; (2) core/theme/ag-grid-theme.ts is already a hand-mirrored hex copy (069 §4), so one notation covers both files and 074's job becomes copy-paste; (3) Tailwind 4 resolves bg-primary/10 through color-mix identically for hex and oklch, so no utility changes behaviour. 071 still derives the dark twin in oklch space — it just emits hex.
The additions 22 new names, no --info, no --disabled*, no --on-sub. Full board below. Three POS tokens are deliberately not adopted: --disabled/-ink/-border (we express disabled with disabled:opacity-45 and that idiom is fine), --on-sub (= text-primary-foreground/80), and any --info hue — see the ruling below.
The orphans --ring←--primary; --input←--border-strong; --secondary←POS --key; the four --sidebar* derived (two options, owner picks). The sidebar is the one surface PosTheme has no answer for at all — a till has no nav rail. Both derivations are rendered side by side in §3.
Ruling — there is no --info token. POS declares no info hue, and our 15 raw blue/sky-* sites (069 §3a) are informational states. Now that --primary is steel blue, an info token would be a second blue three shades from the first — two tokens the eye cannot tell apart and the reader cannot choose between. Informational states use --primary / --primary-050. This is a constraint handed to 077: it inherits four severity families, not five.
Naming reconciliation. 072 wrote --danger-800; our existing token is --destructive (14 uses). Rather than run two spellings, the severity family is named --success / --attention / --danger — each with a -050 ground, a -border and an -800 ink — and --destructive: var(--danger) is kept as a compatibility alias so the 14 existing call sites and shadcn idiom keep working. Zero churn, one symmetric family. --primary takes the same four tiers, which is what makes the pill rail's four severities (ok / go / warn / bad) mechanical.

4 · What this table does not fix

  • 249 raw palette occurrences in 41 files (danger 87 · warning 75 · success 55 · info 15 · misc 4 · b/w 13), across 41 files, ≈165 distinct decisions after dark: twins. They are Tailwind palette classes (bg-amber-500/15, text-red-700) and will keep rendering Tailwind's hues on a POS ground until swept. → 077, which this table now gives four families and four tiers to sweep into.
  • core/theme/ag-grid-theme.ts — 9 colour params × 2 modes, a hand-mirrored hex copy by design ("AG params don't resolve CSS vars in all paths"). 6 grids. Gets nothing for free. → 074. The --grid-* values in this mock's .pal-pos are a suggestion to 074, not a decision.
  • Two cellStyle hex overrides — deliveries/columns.ts:46 and document/columns.ts:190, both #c62828. Inside the grid, bypassing even the grid theme. Shown red in §2.
  • The two brand surfaces — LoginPage navy panel + gold kicker, HomePage navy hero + gold kicker, and their four paired text-white* classes. Navy #002554 against steel #2F63A6 is the collision. → 075. BrandMark is colour-agnostic by construction and survives either way (069 §5).
  • Dark mode. PosTheme declares no dark values at all. Every .dark value in global.css is still warm near-black until 071 derives the twin — which is why the rollout is palette-light → palette-dark → screen.
