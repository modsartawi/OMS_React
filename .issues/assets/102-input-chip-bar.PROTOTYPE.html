The run strip: collapse, expand, edit, and the stale run

101 approved the strip and placed it; this prototype settles how it behaves. Its one structural proposal is a single status slot in the strip that is empty when the run on screen matches the inputs, says inputs changed when it does not, and becomes Processing… while a run is out — so stale, in-flight and settled are one slot in three states, not three inventions. Every value is a real 098 capture (03-applied-and-potential, and 04a-unknown-material for the 400).

Settled — the inputs on screen are the inputs that produced these results, so the status slot is absent, not reassuring. Five chips (the chip-set switcher adds the three test levers and the pricing-elements flag). Plant, org, channel and date are chipped even when they hold their defaults — 098 finding 8 (an invalid plant prices silently) means the determination the run actually used must be visible without expanding anything. The levers are the opposite: blank ⇒ no chip, per 100 §4.

Expanded. The form replaces the collapsed row in place — the chips, the money and the buttons are all inside it, so nothing below moves except by the form's own height, and the results stay exactly where the eye left them. Focus lands on Plant, the first field. Esc collapses and returns focus to the chip set; Ctrl+Enter processes from anywhere. Note the money readout is gone, not moved while the form is open: the total belongs to a run, and you are no longer looking at that run's inputs.

Stale. Item 10's quantity has been typed up to 3; the results still describe the ×2 run — look at the Items row against the line's 2 EA. Three treatments on the switcher. A (drawn) marks it in the slot where the change happened and once at the top of the work area. B is today: the screen says nothing and the analyst reads a total for a basket that no longer exists. C dims the money instead of speaking. Note what none of them do: no re-run, no block, no discard — that is behaviour, and the map's scope line puts it outside this effort.

In flight. Same slot, next state. The previous results stay on screen — 098's runs return in 184–268 ms, so blanking them would be a flicker of nothing, and the analyst's whole loop is comparing this total with the last one. The inputs, Edit ▾, Clear and ⛁ Wipe cache lock (as they do today); a hairline runs along the strip's own bottom edge. The spinner is proposed to appear only after 150 ms, so an ordinary run never flashes.

The run failed. The strip stays collapsed — a failed Process is still a Process, and auto-expanding would move the screen at the moment the analyst starts reading the error. The money readout is absent because there is no total. Instead the banner carries the route: INVALID_UOM is an item fault, so it points at Items; a determination fault (098's bad distribution channel) points at the run settings and opens the form on click.

The seven questions, and what this prototype proposes

QuestionProposal
1 · What the bar says The header only — the five determination chips, plus a lever chip per lever that is set and ELEM on when pricing elements are asked for. Determination fields chip even at their defaults; levers and flags chip only when set. No counts in the strip at all: 5 items and 2 conditions would be summarising a frame that is right there and never collapses. The manual-conditions count rides its own disclosure label inside Items (Manual conditions · 2), where the thing it counts actually lives.
2 · What expands it The chip set is the control — one <button>, one tab stop, one aria-expanded, with Edit ▾ as its visible tail for discoverability (100 §3 forbids the chips themselves acting). Expansion replaces the row in place, per 101. Collapse: the same control, Esc, or Process.
3 · Auto-collapse Collapses on Process — every Process, including one that 400s. It never auto-expands: the screen must not move itself while the analyst is reading a failure. The 400 banner instead offers an explicit route into whichever region caused it.
4 · Staleness Treatment A: the slot says inputs changed, the work area says once what it is describing. Neutral by force, not by taste — 100 spent the whole hue budget on success and attention, so amber here would both break that budget and promise a fault where there is none. Derived from a pure comparison of the current request against the request that produced the on-screen result — a testable seam, and the one the spec should name.
5 · Items & manual conditions Neither collapses, neither joins the strip (099's two-lifetimes ruling). Manual conditions stay a disclosure inside the Items frame — no fourth frame (101 §4) — carrying a count when non-empty, and opening itself when rows exist, so a manual condition can never be silently in effect behind a closed twisty.
6 · Keyboard Expand → focus Plant. Esc → collapse, focus returns to the chip set. Ctrl+Enter → Process from anywhere, including inside the items grid, which is what makes the tweak-one-field-and-re-run loop mouse-free. Process keeps focus across the collapse because it is in the strip in both states.
7 · In flight The slot's third state. Previous results stay; inputs and the two quiet verbs lock; a hairline on the strip's bottom edge; the spinner waits 150 ms so a 268 ms run doesn't flash. Edit ▾ is disabled rather than hidden — hiding it would reflow the strip twice per run.

What this prototype is asking you to rule

  • The status slot itself. One slot, three states — or should staleness and in-flight live in different places? The slot is drawn dashed and neutral deliberately: it is the only new form on the screen, and it is not a chip (it changes while you read it, which is exactly what 100's chip test excludes).
  • Which staleness treatment — A, B or C. C is the quiet one and costs the analyst the readable total; B is honest about doing nothing and leaves a lie on screen.
  • Whether defaults earn chips. Drawn yes. The alternative — chip only what differs from the default — halves the strip on most runs and makes the strip's length meaningful, at the price of not being able to see what the run actually used.
  • Whether a failed run may auto-expand. Drawn no, with a route. Say if the determination-fault case should just open the form.

Deliberately not settled here

The per-line expansion and the elements trace (103), the line's columns and the missed card's fields (104), where the strip wraps and the 66/34 stacks (105), and mirroring (106) — though note the strip has three wrap groups (chips · money · controls) and 105 inherits that.
