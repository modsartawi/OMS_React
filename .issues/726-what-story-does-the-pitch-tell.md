# 726 — What story does the pitch tell?

## Question

What is the **one story** the pitch tells, and what do we ask management for at the end?

Questions to settle:
- **The spine.** For example, follow one customer's order across channels, through insurance,
  stock from a donor store, a courier callout and SAP posting, with each executive's payoff
  called out along the way. Or use a different spine.
- **The order of the argument.** Market → our coverage → where we lead → proof numbers → cost of
  buying versus building → headline features → roadmap → ask.
- **The ask.** Budget, headcount, a mandate to retire a vendor, or recognition and continued
  funding.
- **The CMO and CFO payoff.** The owner chose the four headline leads from operations and compliance only (724), and declined to headline loyalty, Wasfaty/NPHIES scale and the SAP audit trail. The story still has to tell the CMO and CFO what is in it for them. Decide where their payoff comes from: framing the operations leads, the proof numbers, the headline features (725) or the roadmap.
- **Who presents what**, and how long the slot is.

The answer is the narrative outline: section by section, which claim, which proof (a matrix
row, a number from 723, a demo moment) and which executive it is aimed at. That outline unblocks
the deck and demo-script work, which is still listed under Not yet specified.

## Answer

Grilled with the owner on 2026-11-03. The owner chose the recommended option on three pivotal questions and accepted the outline and the settled decisions below.

**Pivotal rulings (owner):**
- **The ask: a mandate plus a funded roadmap.** Management formally names our platform the **OMS of record**, so no Manhattan or Fluent purchase. Management funds the ranked roadmap with a **named headcount**. The cost-of-buying ticket (722) frames that headcount as a fraction of a licence.
- **The spine: one order plus live numbers.** Open on the command centre's live numbers. Follow one Wasfaty order end to end. Close on Customer 360 and the copilot.
- **The slot: 45 minutes, owner presents everything.** About 30 minutes of story and demo, then 15 of Q&A. One voice throughout. Every live moment has a recorded fallback.

- **CMO payoff:** Customer 360, the own-channel share and the loyalty-identified rate. Loyalty stays out of the headline leads, as ruled in 724.
- **Kept out of the demo:** insurer rules with the playground, and prescription sessions. Neither will be live by 3 December (724), so both appear only as roadmap claims.
- **Callouts and the scorecard** are expected live by 3 December (724). Their hop still needs a recorded fallback.
- **Numbers on screen keep their caveats (723).** "Value through our tills" is never called revenue. Our online share is never set against Nahdi's digital share as like-for-like.
- **Delivery-speed numbers** for the "late order" moment depend on 727. Without them, the copilot story is told from the order's own timeline only.
